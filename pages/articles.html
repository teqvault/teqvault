Transmissions

Articles & Devlogs

Deep dives into our engineering processes, transmedia world-building, software development, and IP design philosophy.

This is where the engineering gets documented and the lore gets dissected. Our articles and devlogs pull back the curtain on how games and software are actually built at Teq Vault — pipeline decisions, design choices we stand by, and the ones we had to undo.

Topics range from test-driven development in match-3 combat systems, to writing antagonists that operate on pure logic, to prototyping text-driven interfaces for low-resource hardware. The goal is the same in every piece: explain the work in enough detail that another engineer or writer could argue with it.

Published pieces are free to read. A small number of drafts may still show as in progress. New transmissions appear as the studio ships milestones.
