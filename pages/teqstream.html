By Design

No account. No server. No catch.

TeqStream has no sign-in screen, because it has nothing to sign in to. There is no Teq Vault server behind it collecting what you watch.

Watch progress, favorites, and playlists live on your device and go nowhere else. When you stream, your phone talks directly to the Internet Archive or the broadcaster — we are not in the middle of it, and we do not see it.

No third-party ad or analytics SDK · Privacy details

About the catalog

TeqStream provides access to third-party content that Teq Vault does not host, curate, or rate. Live channels and archive material can include news footage, horror, and other adult themes, and individual streams can go offline without notice. Viewer discretion is advised.

Everything in one player

TeqStream is in testing. When it launches on Google Play, it will play what is already on your phone and open a library of freely licensed film, television, and literature alongside it.
