Introduction

Welcome to Teq Vault LLC ("we," "our," or "us"), publishing mobile games and software under the name Average Jude Games. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (teqvault.online), use our mobile applications — including TeqMusic — and interact with our associated platforms.

Information We Collect

  • Log Data: Like many website operators, we collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our site. This may include your computer's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our site that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and other statistics.
  • Contact Information: If you use our contact forms, we collect your name, email address, and any message content you provide to respond to your inquiries.
  • Account Information: Some of our apps, including TeqMusic, require an account to use. Creating one collects your email address and a securely hashed password, handled through our authentication provider, Supabase. We never store your password in plain text and do not have direct access to it.

Cookies and Web Beacons

We use cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the site visitor accesses, and to personalize or customize our web page content based upon visitors' browser type or other information.

Google AdSense and DoubleClick Cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Users may opt-out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings webpage.

TeqMusic Specifically

TeqMusic is a music player app that combines music you already own with a curated catalog published by Teq Vault LLC. Because of how it works, it collects and stores some information beyond what's described above:

  • Account data: Your email address and authentication credentials, used solely to sign you in and keep your data (playlists, listening history) tied to your account.
  • Media library access: With your permission, TeqMusic reads audio files already stored on your device so you can play them in the app. These files are read locally for playback only — they are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party, unless you are an app administrator specifically using an internal content-management feature to add tracks to the curated catalog.
  • Listening activity: We record which tracks you play and how long you listen, tied to your account, to power features like your personal "Most Played" list and to determine when narrative content ("lore") tied to a curated track unlocks for you.
  • Playlists: Playlists you create, and which tracks you add to them, are stored under your account so they're available whenever you sign in.
  • Downloaded content: If you download a curated track for offline listening, the audio file is stored locally on your device. This is separate from, and does not affect, your device's local music library.
  • Backend provider: TeqMusic's account system and app data are hosted by Supabase. Supabase processes this data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. See Supabase's own privacy policy for details on their practices as a processor.

TeqMusic does not currently include a third-party advertising SDK. In-app promotional content for other Teq Vault products (e.g., a "More from Teq Vault" section) links out to our own websites or store listings and does not involve any ad network or additional data collection beyond standard link-tap analytics, if enabled.

Children's Information

If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website or through one of our apps, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
