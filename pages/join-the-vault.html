Operation: Quality Control

Help Us Refine the Future

We are looking for detail-oriented minds to stress-test our digital worlds, polish our narrative lore, and push our software to its limits.

Teq Vault is still early. That means volunteer roles, remote collaboration, and credit on the products and IP you help improve — not a traditional payroll career page. If you care about finding the bug that only appears on one Android skin, or the lore inconsistency that breaks immersion on a second reading, this is for you.

Applications are reviewed as capacity allows. Strong reports, clear reproduction steps, and specific feedback matter more than a long resume. Successful testers and proofreaders often move into tighter circles of the project as the studio grows.

The Circuit-Level Standard

"We don't just fix typos or find glitches — we optimize the infrastructure of immersion. If you have an engineering mindset for creativity and care deeply about quality, we want you in the Vault."

Game testers focus on mechanics, balance, and device-specific issues across live and in-development titles. Proofreaders work through manuscripts and in-game text for clarity, consistency, and lore fidelity. Software beta testers exercise TeqXus and TeqDocs under real conditions — browsers, files, edge cases, and AI response quality.

All roles are remote. At this stage they are volunteer, with product and IP credit where contribution is material. Expectations are written into each application path so you know what “good” looks like before you apply.
