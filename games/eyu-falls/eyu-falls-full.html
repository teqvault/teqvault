HOW THIS WORKS

On the board: tap to rotate · swipe left/right to move · swipe down to drop (a short swipe soft-drops, a long fast swipe hard-drops) · swipe up to hold.

Clear lines to fill your elemental pools (top row) — the color of the blocks you clear decides which pool fills.

Once you've banked enough of a color, its matching Pack ability (bottom row) lights up. Tap it to spend the pool and trigger the effect.

Different missions can change what each ability does — check the tooltip on any ability for its current effect.
